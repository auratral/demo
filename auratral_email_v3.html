Auratral is India’s first Compute-to-Data hosting platform built specifically for AI Healthcare and Medical Research. We host high-fidelity clinical datasets—including longitudinal EHRs, DICOM medical imaging, and genomics—within secure data vaults. Instead of transferring sensitive medical records, we provide approved researchers with a VPC-isolated sandbox next to the data. Train diagnostic models, run statistical pipelines, and export only final weights and validation metrics.
How access works
1Ethics Clearance
Verify IRB/IEC Medical Credentials
Provide your approved Institutional Review Board (IRB) ethics protocol number and sponsoring medical institution credentials. Sign the Clinical Data Privacy NDA before access.
2Sandbox Provisioned
Mount Chosen Medical Datasets
A dedicated VPC-isolated container spins up with your chosen medical dataset (e.g., oncology scans, cardiology EHR) mounted read-only. A web IDE loads in-browser instantly.
3Safe Export
Export AI Weights & Research Metrics
Train diagnostic algorithms, validate research hypotheses, or run clinical analytics. Export only final model weights (.pkl / .onnx) and metrics. Raw records never leave the vault.
🔒
Runtime-Enforced Medical Data Governance
Sandboxes run in isolated Virtual Private Clouds with blocked outbound network traffic. Medical datasets are mounted strictly read-only. Outgoing files undergo automated static scans to prevent raw patient record leakage—guaranteeing DPDP, HIPAA, and GDPR compliance at the infrastructure level.
